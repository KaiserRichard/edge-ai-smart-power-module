### A3 text measurement and assessment limits

The PDF's extracted technical-text font size is about **6.017 pt** on pages 1, 2, 3, 5, and 6. Its absolute minimum on these pages is **5.132 pt**, principally drawing-grid annotations, which should not be confused with component-value text. Sheet03 has essential component references, values, and signal names at **5.014 pt**, with some notes at 5.113 pt; this is approximately 17% smaller than the other sheets' technical text. These are PDF text-object font sizes, not a measurement of the visible stroke's cap height.

At A3 scale, the 6 pt technical text is small but generally decipherable where unobstructed. Sheet03's approximately 5 pt technical text is marginal for comfortable paper review; reducing a printed sheet further would worsen it. Several values are actually overprinted, independent of the chosen zoom or printing resolution. An actual physical A3 print was not produced, so physical print readability is **not validated**. No numerical minimum text-size requirement was supplied; font size alone is not used as a claimed standards violation.

For the table below, **text PASS** means the reviewed technical text is unobscured and visually readable; confirmed wire/symbol/frame overprints produce a local text FAIL even when a reader can infer the intended string. **Flow** assesses the primary signal/power sequence; **blocks** assesses named functional grouping; **labels** assesses sparse/useful net naming rather than text placement (placement defects are counted under text).
