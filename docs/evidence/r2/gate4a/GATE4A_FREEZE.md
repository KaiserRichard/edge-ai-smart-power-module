## OWNER EXPLANATION — one page

The fixed owner choices are D test board, normal input **9–26 V**, D101 **SMCJ36CA**, F101 **Schurter 3403.0285.23**, and an **isolated 48→24 V module upstream**. Its exact MPN, source C/L/current, tolerance and surge behavior remain OPEN; isolation does not limit fault energy. **J502 is PENDING** under `feat/v1a-j502-connector`. No Pi/SBC before fault tests and all applicable gates pass.

The buck high-side fault energizes L201 and the raw bank before the output pair opens. The protected crowbar then handles only the downstream bank/harness; Q330 handles the raw bank and L201 tail. Conditional energy is 15.217 mJ new work, 99.865 mJ raw disposal, and 174.086 mJ across raw/protected/2.2 mF dummy stores. Those numbers depend on ≤30 µF local input C, ≤28.289211 V source, ≤100 µC positive replenishment and the qualified magnetic envelope. They are not component pulse ratings.

The source needs a physical fault-onset→current-off ≤10 µs discriminator. Bulk OV alone can take 19–36 µs even before propagation; a missing discriminator cannot be cured by assigning a fixture-triggered timing PASS. Complete comparator/driver/current timing, powered-off sense, bootstrap and held-rail behavior require real pin circuits and named dummy-load measurements. Their worst-case values stay OPEN until evidence exists.

The retained protected bank is 1.88 mF nominal / 3.5 mΩ catalog ESR. The inherited 0.5 mΩ timing contract is invalid; its 5.713245 V sensitivity and historical 5.542140 V harness counterexample remain visible. A test board may investigate this candidate, but strict far-end `peak+Upeak<5.5 V` and normal `minimum−Umin≥4.75 V at 5 A` govern acceptance.

Paper defects, actual measurements and incomplete construction are distinguished in the resolution table. Each unmeasured behavior has a named test with a PASS criterion and prerequisites. Missing fixture hardware, uncertainty, source construction or BOM proof keeps this package BLOCKED. Passing arithmetic checks does not remove those blocks.
