All 100 nF bypass capacitors added here are **GRM188R71H104KA93D**, 50 V X7R 0603. R370/R371 are **TNPW060310K0BEEA**; R372/R373/R376 are **TNPW0603100KBEEA**; R374/R375 are **TNPW0603220KBEEA**. Added capacitors are counted in the relevant supply's load/energy. None adds unexplained capacitance across the protected output.

Q304/Q305/Q306 are Nexperia **2N7002,215**, SOT-23 pins 1 gate,2 source,3 drain. Q305 drain=main SHDN/source=GND, gate biased by R324/R325 100k/100k VIN_FUSED→gate→GND and D305 BZT52H-C10,115 (cathode gate/anode GND). Q306 drain=Q305 gate/source=GND/gate=MAIN_PERMISSION, R326 100k gate–GND. Q304 drain=SHDN/source=GND/gate=FAULT_REQUEST. Independent resistor worst case at 9 V gives gate=9×0.9975/(1.0025+0.9975)=**4.48875 V**, before leakage. The published 2N7002 4.5 V RDS(on) condition is not reached at that corner; cold/hot SHDN sink margin is **OPEN**, not a transplanted 10 V RDS limit. TPS2663 SHDN source current is internally limited; establish its applicable maximum and threshold margin before construction acceptance.

The remaining **MAIN_PERMISSION, SERIES_RUN, CLEAR_FAULT_N, fresh ARM, safe-energy and persistent-short integrity producers are explicit construction blockers**. The old inherited logic is not certified by naming these signals. Before capture, their complete connection equations must enforce the state table in TIMING_AND_LOGIC.md. No measurement can establish an unwired producer. T15 is the qualification after the producer circuit, package pins and default pulls are reviewable. Reset while a permanent buck short remains must not reconnect energy merely because SW is currently low with the eFuse off; a reviewed pre-arm integrity check is needed. Test-board manual continuity checks alone do not establish an autonomous feature.

For each operating IC estimate `TJ = TA + P·thetaJA` only at the manufacturer's board condition, then measure actual temperature and bound junction with the applicable board model. For U318, `P=(12.31−3.3)·I3V3`, e.g. **180.2 mW at an assumed 20 mA**; this is a load-budget screen, not a specified current requirement. Actual summed max quiescent/switching current and thermal load remain T07/T15.

Hot testing heats components locally: LM4040 I-grade qualification ≤85°C; ADG4612 BRUZ grade ≤105°C; UCC27511 junction ≤140°C; LTC I-grade operating junction ≤125°C. WFMB terminals must start ≤85°C and stay <95°C; 8521 trunk ≤80°C, 8503 wire ≤105°C, selected Harwin housing ≤85°C. With provisional temperature uncertainty ±2°C, measured targets are respectively ≤83/<93, ≤78, ≤103, ≤83°C; actual uncertainty governs. MOSFET, inductor and other component ratings require their exact datasheet/thermal model. No blanket 85°C ambient test is implied; minimum ambient and whole-product service envelope are OPEN owner requirements.
