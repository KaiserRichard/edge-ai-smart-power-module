## F2-01 — completed J502 handoff (R4A-11/16)

**DECISION:** use solution A from commit `41cb9e889418d50d2f5f24467e66ac7f15a90476`: Phoenix 1776508 board header + 1777989 plug, TE 3-87456-6 full 2×20 housing and ten 87309-9 sockets, Belden 8521 002100/010100 AWG16 75 mm trunks, and Belden 8502 002100/010100 AWG20 branches (2×15 mm positive; 8×35 mm return). J502.1=LOAD_5V1; .2=GND. Pi physical +2/4 and GND6/9/14/20/25/30/34/39 remain fixed. Supersede Harwin and the interim 8503 selection. Molex solution B is a documented fallback, not fitted in parallel.

**CALCULATION:** retain architecture D and the 13.6 mΩ BSC034 pair screen: `Rloop=34.255546+13.6−6=41.855546 mΩ`. At source screen 5.026143255 V, `Vfar=5.026143255−5×0.041855546−0.020=4.796865525 V`. Reserve above 4.75 V is 46.866 mV, or 31.866 mV after the proposed 15 mV measurement allowance. A 100 mV trough gives 4.716865525 V and fails. These remain conditional allocations, not a guaranteed hot assembly. Each positive branch must be rated above its measured current; matching ratio ≤1.05 gives `5×1.05/2.05=2.560976 A` in the lower-resistance leg. The preliminary fully loaded TE derating screen near 2.01 A does not qualify 5 A operation.

**T11 acceptance:** complete hot J502 pole ≤3.12 mΩ, each Pi mated-contact/female-crimp block ≤20 mΩ, all fan-out splice joints together ≤0.5 mΩ per polarity, full loop/voltage with actual uncertainty, individual contact current below applicable hot male/female rating, and verified indexed carrier/crimp/tooling/strain relief. The full housing is power-only: no host telemetry connection until its separately reviewed mapping is incorporated. An open positive contact leaves 5 A on one pin and is not qualified. Test first with a rated dummy coupon; no Pi is required to capture the board.

**Disposition:** connector selection and wire mismatch are closed for the test-board candidate. Supplier mixed-mate applicability, hot measurements, splice process and actual Pi header remain T11/T16 assembly/integration gates. They no longer appear as a missing J502 selection.

## F2-02 — controlled auxiliary startup (R4A-07/14/15/16)

**DECISION:** insert U327 `TPS259470LRPWR` after D304 and before the existing DRV_HOLD bank. Preserve D304 reverse blocking. U327's R374/C374 network (100 Ω + 1 µF to ground) slows charging instead of demanding that U317 charge 440 µF in its typical 9 ms soft-start. S03-18 gives every RPW-10 pin and exact resistor/capacitor identity. There is no fitted fast-charge bypass. C350 is selected as the identified 10 nF C0G for the isolated test candidate; the −1 stopper continues to latch off rather than being given a long unreviewed fault timer.

U316 ENOUT now clamps U317 AUX_RUN until the stopper output approaches its input. R345 `TNPW0805464KBEEA` (464 kΩ) from AUX_LIMITED and R346 `TNPW0603100KBEEA` (100 kΩ) to GND set RUN. ENOUT is open drain; at AUX_LIMITED≤35 V and RUN=0.8 V the sink demand is less than 74 µA, below its 0.1 mA table point (maximum low 0.8 V). U317 has a nominal 1.21 V enable threshold; its complete RUN hysteresis/leakage transfer remains T07A. This prevents the earlier undefined RUN connection and allows a settled stopper before converter startup. During a large voltage-clamping event ENOUT may inhibit U317: the held rail must complete disposal, not promise indefinite operation at 58.1 V.

C352/C377 are two 10 µF output ceramics; C353/C379 two 4.7 µF internal VCC ceramics. Their respective effective minima are 10 µF and 4.7 µF, verified before startup. C351 remains the 4.7 µF input candidate with an effective ≥4.7 µF requirement: if its measured value fails, add/resize before energization; nominal alone does not meet this bound. C378 supplies U316 with 1 µF/100 V bypass; C380 is the separate U317 VIN 100 nF bypass. The FE-20 pins are explicitly assigned in S03-13; PGND pins/EP, VCC/PVCC, RUN and the two bootstraps are distinguished.

**CALCULATION:** nominal charging slope from the manufacturer's equation is `2000/Cdvd[pF] V/ms = 2 V/s`. The pin-current/C sensitivity at 3.82 µA and a separately qualified 0.5 µF floor is 7.64 V/s; this is not a guaranteed external slew transfer. T07D requires measured slope ≤10 V/s under initial, partial-charge, hot-plug and recovery conditions. With **qualification allocations** of total charged capacitance≤600 µF and control current≤40 mA, `Iout≤C·SR+Icontrol=46 mA`. At AUX_LIMITED≥7.5 V, efficiency≥75%, U327 input current overhead≤610 µA and a further 5 mA converter/branch allocation, `Ibranch≤12.311325×(0.046+0.000610)/(7.5×0.75)+0.005=107.014375 mA`. The transferred LT4363 table floor is `45 mV/(0.33Ω×1.01)=135.013501 mA`, giving 27.999126 mA conditional reserve. The table test point is not an invented 9 V guarantee. Actual branch current, converter local-capacitor startup, leakage and efficiency are T07A/C/D.

The charger EN rising screen is 9.968614…10.471759 V; its OVLO rising screen 14.087415…14.772620 V, including ±0.25% resistor screens and ±0.1 µA input current. These are upstream-of-U327 voltages. Both dividers keep enable/OVLO pins in their proper low-voltage domains at the bounded normal input. U327's lowest current-limit setting is approximately 0.5 A, **not a 46 mA current limiter**; the ramp controls inrush while U316 remains the branch-current protection. ITIMER is NC for minimum overcurrent blanking. AUXOFF/FLT are NC outputs with probe pads; they are not silently pulled to a potentially 5.25 V DET5 rail when their recommended ceiling is 5 V.

**Thermal limit:** assigning the full 12.311325 V drop and 46 mA simultaneously to U327 gives 0.566321 W. The datasheet's 74.5°C/W board-dependent sensitivity gives 127.191°C at 85°C ambient, over its 125°C operating ceiling. Initial U327 coupon local ambient is therefore **≤70°C**, with the same sensitivity 112.191°C. This is an initial test restriction, not a new product ambient requirement. Actual PCB thermal correlation and the measured Vdrop×I trajectory decide T07D; extend hot testing only after that review. C374 must retain ≥0.5 µF at up to VIN+5 V (screen 17.312 V), within its 25 V rating. Include charge-pump startup pulses in the measured 40 mA allocation rather than asserting they are zero.

**Additional timer correction:** LT4363 normally biases TMR around 0.5 V. The historical `C×1.335/310µA` expression assumed a zero initial voltage. Correct OC timing is `C×(Voff−Vstart)/ITMR`; at 10 nF, Voff=1.375 V, Vstart=0.5 V and 310 µA it is 28.226 µs. With −5% C and 1.335 V off threshold it is 25.589 µs **conditional on that initial voltage and test-point current**, not a full minimum. OV has a separate warning phase/current and cannot use this OC equation. Record actual initial TMR and stress-dependent charging; no 43 µs guaranteed survival or startup time is retained.

**Disposition:** the held-bank charging topology and timer candidate are concrete selections for an isolated coupon/test-board candidate. T07D owns their application-dependent closure. This removes the missing charger choice; it does not certify startup, pass-FET hot SOA, compensation, or the separate fast rail-loss/state circuit. Only those still absent paper connections can block capture; actual waveform/thermal limits are pre-energization tests with physically removable main-power links.
