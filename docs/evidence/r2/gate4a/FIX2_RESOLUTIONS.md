## F2-02 — controlled auxiliary startup (R4A-07/14/15/16)

**DECISION:** insert U327 `TPS259470LRPWR` after D304 and before the existing DRV_HOLD bank. Preserve D304 reverse blocking. U327's R374/C374 network (100 Ω + 1 µF to ground) slows charging instead of demanding that U317 charge 440 µF in its typical 9 ms soft-start. S03-18 gives every RPW-10 pin and exact resistor/capacitor identity. There is no fitted fast-charge bypass. C350 is selected as the identified 10 nF C0G for the isolated test candidate; the −1 stopper continues to latch off rather than being given a long unreviewed fault timer.

U316 ENOUT now clamps U317 AUX_RUN until the stopper output approaches its input. R345 `TNPW0805464KBEEA` (464 kΩ) from AUX_LIMITED and R346 `TNPW0603100KBEEA` (100 kΩ) to GND set RUN. ENOUT is open drain; at AUX_LIMITED≤35 V and RUN=0.8 V the sink demand is less than 74 µA, below its 0.1 mA table point (maximum low 0.8 V). U317 has a nominal 1.21 V enable threshold; its complete RUN hysteresis/leakage transfer remains T07A. This prevents the earlier undefined RUN connection and allows a settled stopper before converter startup. During a large voltage-clamping event ENOUT may inhibit U317: the held rail must complete disposal, not promise indefinite operation at 58.1 V.

**Superseded value:** F2-05 replaces R345 with 383 kΩ after including ENOUT leakage. The paragraph above records the recovered F2-02 decision and its original limitation.

C352/C377 are two 10 µF output ceramics; C353/C379 two 4.7 µF internal VCC ceramics. Their respective effective minima are 10 µF and 4.7 µF, verified before startup. C351 remains the 4.7 µF input candidate with an effective ≥4.7 µF requirement: if its measured value fails, add/resize before energization; nominal alone does not meet this bound. C378 supplies U316 with 1 µF/100 V bypass; C380 is the separate U317 VIN 100 nF bypass. The FE-20 pins are explicitly assigned in S03-13; PGND pins/EP, VCC/PVCC, RUN and the two bootstraps are distinguished.

**CALCULATION:** nominal charging slope from the manufacturer's equation is `2000/Cdvd[pF] V/ms = 2 V/s`. The pin-current/C sensitivity at 3.82 µA and a separately qualified 0.5 µF floor is 7.64 V/s; this is not a guaranteed external slew transfer. T07D requires measured slope ≤10 V/s under initial, partial-charge, hot-plug and recovery conditions. With **qualification allocations** of total charged capacitance≤600 µF and control current≤40 mA, `Iout≤C·SR+Icontrol=46 mA`. At AUX_LIMITED≥7.5 V, efficiency≥75%, U327 input current overhead≤610 µA and a further 5 mA converter/branch allocation, `Ibranch≤12.311325×(0.046+0.000610)/(7.5×0.75)+0.005=107.014375 mA`. The transferred LT4363 table floor is `45 mV/(0.33Ω×1.01)=135.013501 mA`, giving 27.999126 mA conditional reserve. The table test point is not an invented 9 V guarantee. Actual branch current, converter local-capacitor startup, leakage and efficiency are T07A/C/D.

The charger EN rising screen is 9.968614…10.471759 V; its OVLO rising screen 14.087415…14.772620 V, including ±0.25% resistor screens and ±0.1 µA input current. These are upstream-of-U327 voltages. Both dividers keep enable/OVLO pins in their proper low-voltage domains at the bounded normal input. U327's lowest current-limit setting is approximately 0.5 A, **not a 46 mA current limiter**; the ramp controls inrush while U316 remains the branch-current protection. ITIMER is NC for minimum overcurrent blanking. AUXOFF/FLT are NC outputs with probe pads; they are not silently pulled to a potentially 5.25 V DET5 rail when their recommended ceiling is 5 V.

**Thermal limit:** assigning the full 12.311325 V drop and 46 mA simultaneously to U327 gives 0.566321 W. The datasheet's 74.5°C/W board-dependent sensitivity gives 127.191°C at 85°C ambient, over its 125°C operating ceiling. Initial U327 coupon local ambient is therefore **≤70°C**, with the same sensitivity 112.191°C. This is an initial test restriction, not a new product ambient requirement. Actual PCB thermal correlation and the measured Vdrop×I trajectory decide T07D; extend hot testing only after that review. C374 must retain ≥0.5 µF at up to VIN+5 V (screen 17.312 V), within its 25 V rating. Include charge-pump startup pulses in the measured 40 mA allocation rather than asserting they are zero.

**Additional timer correction:** LT4363 normally biases TMR around 0.5 V. The historical `C×1.335/310µA` expression assumed a zero initial voltage. Correct OC timing is `C×(Voff−Vstart)/ITMR`; at 10 nF, Voff=1.375 V, Vstart=0.5 V and 310 µA it is 28.226 µs. With −5% C and 1.335 V off threshold it is 25.589 µs **conditional on that initial voltage and test-point current**, not a full minimum. OV has a separate warning phase/current and cannot use this OC equation. Record actual initial TMR and stress-dependent charging; no 43 µs guaranteed survival or startup time is retained.

**Disposition:** the held-bank charging topology and timer candidate are concrete selections for an isolated coupon/test-board candidate. T07D owns their application-dependent closure. This removes the missing charger choice; it does not certify startup, pass-FET hot SOA, compensation, or the separate fast rail-loss/state circuit. Only those still absent paper connections can block capture; actual waveform/thermal limits are pre-energization tests with physically removable main-power links.

**EVIDENCE:** Texas Instruments [TPS25947 SLVSFC9C, May 2026](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf), RPW pin/order tables and dVdt/hold operation; exact [TPS259470LRPWR](https://www.ti.com/product/TPS25947/part-details/TPS259470LRPWR) identity. **DECISION:** complete the existing candidate network using the LTC3115 voltage-amplifier compensation equations and TA07 values. R343/R344 remain `TNPW060315K0BEEA` (15 kΩ); C356 remains `C1608C0G1H102J080AA` (1 nF); C357 remains `GRM1885C1H330JA01D` (33 pF).

| Feedforward, parallel with R340 | AUX_12V → R344 → AUX_FF → C357 → U317.8 FB |

| RUN | R345 **TNPW0805383KBEEA**, 383 kΩ AUX_LIMITED→AUX_RUN; R346 100 kΩ AUX_RUN→GND; U316.11 and U317.2 both AUX_RUN |

Neither compensation branch returns to ground. No external CPOLE is populated in this candidate. Component values alone do not establish closed-loop stability with the stopper, diode and slow charger attached.

**CALCULATION:** for ideal error-amplifier analysis, `Hcomp(s)=−[R343+1/(sC356)]/[R340 || (R344+1/(sC357))]`. The two network zeros are 10.610330 and 4.751603 kHz; its feedforward pole is 321.525138 kHz. With independent ±0.25% resistor and ±5.18% C0G screens, ranges are 10.062626…11.218015, 4.506326…5.023742 and 304.928056…339.939849 kHz. The assumed 3.76…5.64 µH and **qualification allocation** 10…26 µF effective local output C give LC resonance 13.142982…25.955309 kHz. These are passive-network screens, not gain/phase margins or crossover bounds. The hold bank is downstream of U327 and cannot be inserted directly into that LC calculation.

**RUN correction:** LT4363 lists ±2.5 µA ENOUT leakage at its 80 V test point and 0.8 V maximum low at 0.1 mA. Use a **test allocation**, not a guaranteed application value, of ±3 µA total adverse ENOUT/RUN/PCB current. With U317's 1.16…1.26 V rising threshold and the resistor screen, `Vin,start=VTH(1+Rt/Rb)+Ileak·Rt`. The recovered 464 kΩ design permits 8.531185 V, above the 7.5 V loaded low-line allocation. The selected 383 kΩ gives 4.428769…7.261862 V, leaving 238.138 mV at that upper corner. At AUX_LIMITED≤35 V, even ignoring the lower resistor's helpful current, ENOUT sink is ≤`35/(383k×0.9975)+1µA=92.612844µA` under a separately qualified 1 µA sourced-current allowance.

**OPEN RISK:** the wide lower-start corner does not impose a 6 V UVLO. The reference example's 6 V steady envelope cannot qualify startup below 6 V; T07E must test that ramp interval and the actual converter/stopper current. ENOUT's 0.8 V ceiling can leave U317's internal VCC regulator enabled: it is not a guaranteed full-shutdown level (<0.3 V). Include VCC charging in T07C. Falling hysteresis/current and leakage transfer remain measured quantities.

**T07E disposition:** compensation wiring and the RUN divider are now selected for the isolated candidate. Application loop response, effective capacitance, leakage and low-input startup belong to T07E before integrated energization. They do not independently prohibit capture of isolated test hooks; the absent fast rail-loss/reset circuit still blocks the complete board. No stability or hardware PASS is claimed.

## F2-06 — coupled output-bank allocation (R4A-09/10)

**DECISION:** retain Q330/U330 and twelve 33 mΩ resistors (99 mΩ raw path), Q303/U315 and four 33 mΩ resistors (33 mΩ protected path), C340…343 and the selected negative-return diodes. Before the opposed pair opens, either dump may receive energy from the other output banks. Size its review envelope for that possible transfer unless measured partition and timing justify a smaller bound. The 99.865083 mJ raw-only subtotal remains valid only for its narrower boundary.

**CALCULATION:** `Econnected=Enew+½Craw,max·Vraw,0²+½Cprot,max·Vprot,0²+½Cdummy,max·Vdummy,0²`. Existing raw+source 99.865083 mJ, protected 40.946400 mJ and **effective dummy upper allocation** 2.2 mF at 5.5 V give 174.086483 mJ. A nominal 2.2 mF part at +20% instead gives 180.741483 mJ; measure capacitance before assigning the smaller box. Cable, fixture, other local stores and backfeed are not included in these sensitivities and must be added or physically excluded. Peak magnetic energy is already in Enew and is never added twice.

For each two-string ballast, instantaneous resistor fraction is `(Rj/Sbranch²)/(1/Sa+1/Sb)`. Enumerating independent ±1.35% tolerance/TCR corners gives a maximum 8.618199% for one raw element and 25.507381% for one protected element. Assigning the full 174.086483 mJ pool to each destination **separately** gives 15.003120 mJ/raw element or 44.404903 mJ/protected element. At the larger dummy corner these become 15.576661 / 46.102419 mJ. They are conservative design screens, not simultaneous losses or component ratings. Under the separate 6.881668 V terminal sensitivity, protected ballast alone permits 211.389162 A at 32.5545 mΩ; L201's 78.018 A bound cannot cap this raw-capacitor bypass current.

**Measurement / disposition:** T09C records the coupled interval and both current/energy destinations through final decay, with T13 skew included. Pulse waveform, hot SOA, current sharing and negative commutation require measurement plus applicable ratings; a joule subtotal cannot certify them. Neither an assumed 0.2 J target nor the smaller raw-only resistor energy closes R4A-09. R4A-10 retains the failed four-PCH sensitivity and requires T10 far-end peak/minimum; simply adding capacitance has not been accepted as its repair. An independent numerical integration checks the protected-resistor allocation. The absent rated fixture remains R4A-12.
