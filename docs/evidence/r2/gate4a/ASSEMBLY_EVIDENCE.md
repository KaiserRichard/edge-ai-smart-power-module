**VERDICT BLOCKED.** This is an analysis inventory, not a fabrication BOM. [Baseline assembly index](BASELINE_ASSEMBLY_EVIDENCE.csv) accounts for all 154 inherited references. [Residual passive decisions](RESIDUAL_PASSIVES.csv) select 41 remaining generic/retired references by exact code, value, package and exported-net connection. New active/fixture references are in FREEZE_TABLE.md and PIN_CONTRACT.md. Every reserve must pass collision, pin, polarity and land review at capture. Historical PR15 assignments remain preserved, never silently treated as current qualification.

**DECISION:** retain selected manufacturer identities for U101/U201, Q101 (TI CSD19537Q3), Q102 (onsemi BSS138), REF5025IDGKR, TLV3201AIDBVR, the named LVC logic and INA228/TPS709/TCA9517. Verify each against its manufacturer document in SOURCES.md/independent review, not a package name alone. In particular REF5025 DGK-8 pins: 1 NC,2 VIN=DET_5V,3 TEMP unconnected output,4 GND,5 TRIM/NR per existing reference design (connection/optional noise capacitor review required),6 VOUT=REF_2V5,7/8 NC. U402 TPS709 EN cannot be connected above 6.5 V; inherited telemetry I/O buffers/3V3_LOCAL remain subject to partial-power and isolation review. Missing stress/thermal bounds are OPEN and cannot be replaced by ERC.

**DECISION:** add dummy counterpart JGPIO_FIX **M20-9982046**, manufacturer-specified 40-pin 2.54 mm male, 0.64 mm square/6.1 mm mating length/3 mm THT, tin, 3 A maximum per contact, −40..105°C header body. This defines a dummy endpoint; actual Pi header variants remain unspecified and no Pi is used. Cable housings still govern ≤85°C. Positive pins 2/4 and the eight return contacts are wired as S06-02; verify orientation, insertion depth and the current path before energizing. J502 is **PENDING**, and this header does not resolve that separate connector choice.

C350's unsupported 10 nF code is superseded by verified GRM21BR71H105KA12L **1 µF** in R4A-07, with actual minimum ≥0.75 µF and timer/startup qualification. R304 stays **100 Ω**, CRCW0603100RFKEA (TGUP→gate); TGDN bypasses it for rapid OFF. C305/C306 become 10 µF 50 V 1210; C306 belongs on DRV_HOLD, not a restored 7.5 V LDO. C310/C311/C313 and R314/R315 are DNP with U307. C312/C317 use TDK **C1608C0G1H103J080AA** 10 nF/50 V/±5% C0G0603; C314 **GRM21BR71H224KA01L** 220 nF/50 V/±10% X7R0805. All larger and smaller replacements require changed lands when applicable. RC timers/debounce values are conditional: `tau=RC`, `tdelay=manufacturer function(C)`, not guaranteed edge/race performance.

**Worst-case screens:** generic 50 V bypass on rails bounded ≤35 V has 15 V nameplate headroom; raw-input 58.1 V pins require the selected **100 V** C101/C102/C367, not these 50 V parts. Effective capacitance must still meet the actual local circuit requirements. For standard-mode CRCW0603, P70=0.1 W, film ceiling155°C gives P85≈82.353 mW at the specified board condition; V≤75 V. `P=V²/R` and actual pulse curves govern. A 0603 zero-ohm link is limited to manufacturer 2 A/20 mΩ maximum, so R204 is a signal/configuration link only; it cannot serve JP201/JP202 or any 5 A force path. TNPW0603 max332k and selected precision tolerance/TCR remain mandatory; no generic code outside a family range is accepted.

**Remaining construction prerequisites:** exact rated HV pulser/source boundary, precharge/disconnect/abort circuitry and mechanics; actual isolated module MPN/source/surge envelope; complete state producers; J502 dependency; component-specific hot pulse/SOA evidence for Q330/Q303/D102/D330/ballast; nonlinear L201 envelope; actual probe/DMM serials/calibrations; complete mating/crimp/PCB land drawings. These cannot be made closed by a list of candidate parts. T16 is an incoming and assembled verification after identity/rating evidence and construction are reviewable. T09/T14 prerequisites prohibit unqualified full-energy pulses.
