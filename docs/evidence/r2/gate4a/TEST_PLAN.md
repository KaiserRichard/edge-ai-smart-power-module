**PLAN ONLY; NO TESTS RUN. NOT FOR FABRICATION — ENGINEERING REVIEW REQUIRED.** No Pi/SBC until every applicable normal/fault/telemetry gate passes. Native capture, ERC, layout/DRC/visual review and fabrication-readiness gates precede any built-board work. Gate4a is an R2 analysis task, not the repository R4 fabrication gate.

Equipment: a current-limited programmable 0…30 V bench supply with measured output capacitance; isolated auxiliary fixture/control power; ≥40 W electronic load rated for the reviewed fault voltage/current and a ≥50 W passive load bank; ≥200 MHz oscilloscope, preferably ≥2 GS/s per used channel with sufficient memory and ≥4 analog channels; rated differential probes; DC current probe with a reviewed peak/range and bandwidth sufficient for cutoff plus a higher-bandwidth calibrated current sensor if needed; DMM/four-wire resistance meter; temperature instrumentation at components/ballast terminals and controllable thermal environment; reviewed bounded-energy high-side fault injector, trigger/deskew equipment and physical discharge hardware. A fast voltage scope does not compensate for a slow/saturated current probe. Missing instrument capability or fault-fixture rating is an OPEN precondition.

Obtain its actual Cmin/Cmax/ESR/load response and add those endpoints before releasing Pi integration. Include all dummy C in stored-energy accounting; 2.2 mF at5.5 V adds CALCULATED 33.275 mJ.

Test points/current-probe links are in [TEST_HOOKS.md](TEST_HOOKS.md). Load/probe/fixture changes occur only with supplies off and **both** output banks plus local input/hold banks discharged. **27 V policy:** 27 V is a fault/OVP-window point, outside the owner normal 9…26 V range. Historical initial-only OVP trip is 26.973873…28.289211 V; 27 V can either trip or remain below the fitted unit's threshold. Measure that unit's trip first and record the state. If U101 has opened at27 V, the buck high-side-short run is “source already isolated”; it cannot substitute for an energized27 V run. Do not force OVP defeat in the ordinary matrix. Any fixture feed that bypasses U101 is a separately bounded test and cannot PASS the original main-source path. An OVP boundary sweep beyond27 V is conditional on reviewed fixture/component limits, not approval of a higher normal input.

**Fault modes and escalation:** start with minimum bounded energy, no protected load, and buck disabled; characterize raw-node/inductor/injector behavior. EN-low also drops buck PG and normally prevents series RUN, so this is a raw-only preliminary test. Full D requires a documented initialized armed/PG-valid state and credible fault injection without uncontrolled internal low-side shoot-through. Any fixture-emulated PG/permission must be tagged as synthetic characterization and cannot prove autonomous behavior. Review the live-switch fault mode before using it; there is no instruction to short VIN into a normally switching low-side blindly. Complete both autonomous source-cutoff mode A and fixture-timed conditional-contract mode B; B alone is insufficient. Bound external source C, positive work, injected impedance/current and emergency-stop pulse from reviewed ratings. If the fixture/supply limit or injector opening saves the DUT, record a containment failure or incomplete test, not PASS. Each energy increase follows a review of prior raw data and temperatures.

**Capture, synchronously:** at minimum (1) VOUT_5V1 at raw bulk, (2) VOUT_PROTECTED, (3) far-end dummy-header voltage relative to its **loaded** return, and (4) IL201 through detection, series cutoff, main cutoff and the complete current tail. Use an additional synchronized scope/current channel for IMAIN and Iseries/dump currents; capture source-referenced series/crowbar VGS, SHDN, OV divider/comparator/latch, BUCK_PG, DRV_HOLD/DET5/AUX3V3 and VIN_PROTECTED in supplemental runs. Keep timing references common. Different runs cannot establish a same-event correlation without repeatability evidence. Trigger from injector but timestamp t0 from actual conduction/VL; include pre-trigger initialization and enough post-trigger data to show L201 and both banks discharged, then a longer record for source-off/restart. Fast cutoff/peak and millisecond supply/energy tails need both fast and long records.

**Uncertainty:** calibrate/demagnetize/zero the current probe, verify voltage gain/offset/common-mode error, record bandwidth/sample rate/range/noise, check clipping and current saturation, and deskew current against voltage. Bound error from probe loading, voltage reference, repeatability, timing resolution and bandwidth loss. Undersampling/bandwidth that misses a peak cannot be repaired by an arbitrary numerical guard band. Establish a conservative measurement uncertainty `Upeak` at the actual waveform bandwidth before deciding PASS. Report full-bandwidth peaks and supplemental20 MHz ripple; a20 MHz-filtered peak cannot replace the full-bandwidth safety result. Use rated differential probes; no earth/USB/electronic-load return may short out the real harness return. Preserve raw native waveforms plus exported calibrated data, settings/photos, fixture parasitics and an event-specific energy balance.

**Acceptance (per run, then across the matrix):**

- Strict fault rail: `max_t(Vfar_end_measured(t)) + Upeak < 5.5 V`, including the first edge, cutoff, ringing, reconnect attempt and current tail. Also inspect both positive contacts against loaded return/inter-pin spreading. Equality or an uncertainty interval overlapping5.5 V is not PASS. If `peak−Upeak≥5.5 V`, FAIL is established; intermediate outcomes are inconclusive/BLOCKED. Module voltage alone cannot establish Pi-end PASS.
- Normal rail at5 A: `min_t(Vfar_end_measured(t)) − Umin >=4.75 V` during the specified steady/load-step windows. State the load's settling tolerance and window before testing; do not omit a trough by averaging. Record 9/24/26 V steady, ripple and temperature. No false latch/disposal trips on authorized startup/load steps or26 V operation.
- Conditional source contract: total measured main local C effective10…30 µF, qualified Ldiff5…12 µH through0…100 A/temperature, healthy initial IL≤8 A, physical fault→source-off maximum plus timing uncertainty≤10 µs and positive replenishment integral plus uncertainty≤100 µC. Input OVP threshold alone does not bound VIN amplitude; the actual local waveform/source maximum must satisfy the analyzed box. A single fitted unit's trace does not prove all-lot/hot L bounds.
- Current/energy destinations: capture physical series interruption and both dump currents; demonstrate actual raw-bank/L/local-C energy goes to rated paths. Evaluate each MOSFET VDS/ID/VGS trajectory and `integral(V·I dt)`, each resistor `integral(I²R dt)`, current sharing, component pulse/thermal/repetition limits and negative excursions. A voltage PASS with a destroyed or over-stressed component is not a containment PASS. No avalanche credit without separately applicable evidence.
- Rail/interlock: valid loaded driver/bootstrap/detector conditions through physical disposal/decay; measured hold duration exceeds required maximum plus uncertainty. Default SHDN/series off with missing logic, no false crowbar from PG loss alone, fault wins simultaneous PG loss, no held-ARM/manual-reset reconnection into a persistent fault, no reverse/host backpower. Main-eFuse off must preserve independently powered control separately from whole-input-removal testing.
- Telemetry after faults: continuity/polarity/pulse survival first; four-wire shunt/calibrated V/I/P/E, I2C states and accuracy against declared error/temperature range. Host/SBC connection remains prohibited while other gates are unresolved.

**Results record:** one row per voltage/load/temperature/C/ESR/harness/fault mode, commit/testboard/fixture/instrument revision, calibrated peak±uncertainty/minimum±uncertainty, trigger→endpoints with bounds, charge/energy, actual temperatures, fault/reset outcome, raw-file paths and PASS/FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE with the failed criterion. Record failed traces; do not delete them. An instrumented dummy-board PASS is evidence for its measured conditions; Pi integration additionally requires actual Pi input-range/interface and all repository gates to close.
