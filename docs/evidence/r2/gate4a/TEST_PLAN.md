**27 V policy:** 27 V is a fault/OVP-window point, outside the owner normal 9…26 V range. Historical initial-only OVP trip is 26.973873…28.289211 V; 27 V can either trip or remain below the fitted unit's threshold. Measure that unit's trip first and record the state. If U101 has opened at27 V, the buck high-side-short run is “source already isolated”; it cannot substitute for an energized27 V run. Do not force OVP defeat in the ordinary matrix. Any fixture feed that bypasses U101 is a separately bounded test and cannot PASS the original main-source path. An OVP boundary sweep beyond27 V is conditional on reviewed fixture/component limits, not approval of a higher normal input.

**Fault modes and escalation:** start with minimum bounded energy, no protected load, and buck disabled; characterize raw-node/inductor/injector behavior. Bound external source C, positive work, injected impedance/current and emergency-stop pulse from reviewed ratings. If the fixture/supply limit or injector opening saves the DUT, record a containment failure or incomplete test, not PASS. Each energy increase follows a review of prior raw data and temperatures.
