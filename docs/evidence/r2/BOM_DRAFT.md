- **DISTRIBUTOR (2026-09-30)** applies to every price, stock and lifecycle observation below. Public distributor pages were searched by exact MPN and manufacturer; successful page readbacks supplement indexed results. The evidence register preserves the returned crawl age. These are dated web snapshots, **not live stock reservations or firm quotes**.
- **OPEN** means the value was absent, inaccessible, not an exact manufacturer/MPN match, or not qualified by the input. A missing listing is not zero stock, EOL, or proof that a part does not exist. No lifecycle is inferred from stock. DigiKey “Obsolete” is normalized to EOL; “Not For New Designs” to NRND; Mouser “New Product” to Active with the original label retained. “Discontinued at DigiKey” is a channel restriction, not a manufacturer EOL conclusion.
- **Currency and quantity:** prices are USD per piece for an order of **10 pieces of that exact MPN**, using the greatest displayed applicable price break at or below 10. For example, the 6-piece inductor tier applies to 10 pieces. MOQ above 10 leaves the Q10 price OPEN. Cut tape/bulk/tube/tray are used where readable; reel-only and Digi-Reel service charges are not silently treated as quantity-10 offers. Tax, shipping, duty, PCB fabrication, assembly and test equipment are excluded.
- **Single-source screen:** SINGLE-OBSERVED means exactly one of Mouser/DigiKey/LCSC has a read, positive stock figure for that exact manufacturer/MPN. Other channels can be zero or OPEN; this is a sourcing-risk flag, not proof of exclusive worldwide availability. No alternate MPN has been approved. M/D/L abbreviate Mouser/DigiKey/LCSC.
- **Read limitations:** Mouser returned timeouts, cache misses, regional-currency redirects and incomplete USD tables. Some LCSC/DigiKey pages exposed only partial fields. Later direct-page refreshes hit HTTP 429 rate limits; indexed evidence remains explicitly identified. Every unread price is OPEN in the tables and CSV, with a per-source reason. Lifecycle absent from an excerpt remains OPEN, particularly at LCSC. No account, cart, quote request or purchase was used.
