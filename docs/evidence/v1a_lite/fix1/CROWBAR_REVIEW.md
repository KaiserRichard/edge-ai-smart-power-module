# FIX1 crowbar loop: geometry and unresolved pulse limits

**NOT FOR FABRICATION — ENGINEERING REVIEW REQUIRED**

**OBSERVATION:** SCR K/A/G are (69,33.4)/(66.46,33.4)/(63.92,33.4). C217 +/− are (64,26)/(69,26); C218 +/− are (81,26)/(76,26). The nearest bulk negative has a direct 7.4 mm nominal force corridor to K with nearby stitching; the second return also joins this region. Broad front raw copper and inner raw copper connect both banks, with GND on L2 and B.Cu. The force loop no longer depends only on the small isolated front cathode island of R1. No fuse is between the raw bank and SCR. F301 is downstream in the Pi branch.

**OBSERVATION:** R308 is (73,34.5), outside the actual SCR body. Its ground land (73,33.675) has a 4.019 mm front branch to K; obsolete long bottom sense routing was removed. Gate drive uses the existing bottom branch and a new off-land via at (73,36.3). TP301/302 are 3.1 mm apart. Shared GND fill still joins the authored K branches: this is improved local pickup, not an isolated Kelvin net. Verify at component lands for fast pulse qualification.

**CALCULATION / ASSUMPTION:** a broad parallel force/return-sheet approximation `L ≈ μ0 h l / w`, with 0.2355 mm layer-center spacing, 8–30 mm path and 1–3 mm effective width, gives roughly **0.8–8.9 nH for the idealized board segment**. Fringing, slots, current splitting, vertical transitions, TO-220 leads/package and each capacitor's ESL are omitted. L3-to-L2 separation is 1.0802 mm; L3's nearby bottom ground is the closer return sheet. Thus the idealized number is neither a complete extraction nor a safe bound. Package/lead lengths, ESR/ESL and source cutoff are unspecified; an assumed **10–100 nH total-loop sweep** is a sensitivity study only.

`CROWBAR_SENSITIVITY.json` evaluates that sweep at an ASSUMED 5.5 V raw peak and 0.85 V SCR drop: initial slopes **465 / 186 / 93 / 46.5 A/µs**. A 50 A/µs screen would require ≥93 nH at that drop, or ≥110 nH if no minimum SCR drop is assumed. The selected part's 0.85 V threshold is a maximum model parameter, not a guaranteed minimum drop. **Do not deliberately add uncontrolled trace inductance:** it can worsen clamping and remote overshoot. A lower-inductance layout does not itself close SCR di/dt.

**EVIDENCE:** [ST DocID7475 Rev11](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/tyn612.pdf) conditions the 50 A/µs rating on `IG = 2×IGT`, rise ≤100 ns, specified temperature/repetition. Its 140 A/10 ms and 98 A²s conditions are not blanket approval of a capacitor-discharge waveform. R307 remains 100 Ω and R308 1 kΩ; neither gate rise nor driver current has been measured or bounded by this layout task.

**CALCULATION:** including both 1000 µF bulk parts and all six 47 µF ceramics at an illustrative +20% gives 2.7384 mF and **41.417 mJ at 5.5 V**. Ceramic bias/temperature and bulk ESR/ESL are not characterized. More critically, 5.5 V raw peak and source cutoff are not established; that energy is conditional, not a fault maximum. At an illustrative sustained 5 A, the inherited SCR loss screen is 5 W; 0.1/1/10/100/1000 ms cutoff adds 0.5/5/50/500/5000 mJ of conduction energy. Arbitrary upstream current/hiccup can exceed those scenarios.

**DECISION / OPEN RISK:** gate rise/current, di/dt, peak/I²t, junction pulse temperature, holding/recovery, post-cutoff energy and remote overshoot remain **BLOCKED**. An insulated live-anode heatsink envelope cannot be sized or signed off without the energy/repetition and mechanical requirements. No indefinite conduction or bare 60 K/W ambient operation is accepted. Resolve these with the schematic review and [proposed qualification plan](QUALIFICATION_PLAN.md), before prototype approval. The requested layout correction is recorded; no electrical validation is claimed.
