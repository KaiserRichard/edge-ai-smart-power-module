# FIX1 proposed electrical / thermal qualification plan

**NOT FOR FABRICATION — ENGINEERING REVIEW REQUIRED**

**STATUS: PROPOSED, NOT ENGINEERING-APPROVED.** Source impedance/current limits, enclosure/airflow, ambient, derating, repetition and measurement uncertainty remain OPEN requirements. This plan does not authorize fabrication or fault testing.

1. Complete independent schematic/PCB review, resolve input cutoff/latch/SCR pulse issues, electrical spacing/environment and supplier assembly orientation. Review actual insulated mounting hardware, probe bodies and SCR live-tab/heatsink envelope. An indefinite SCR conduction mode is unacceptable; do not invent a heatsink size without a bounded cutoff/repetition case.
2. Visual/polarity and solder inspection, then unpowered continuity and short checks. Inspect off-land via mask webs and component solder fillets/voids. Confirm J502 pin 1 against the keyed harness mating face. Check the actual test-pad-to-component continuity on both board faces.
3. Current-limited bench supply, no load, verify the output rail; begin with a low dummy/electronic load. No SBC as first load. Raise load in bounded steps only after review acceptance; monitor input FETs, U201, inductor, fuse and connectors continuously.
4. Measure actual efficiency/loss and steady-state temperatures at low/high operating input and the approved ambient/enclosure/airflow. Infer junction temperature with an appropriate calibrated package model, not raw IR case temperature. The historical 50°C ambient / 110°C junction screen is an ASSUMPTION; the present model fails it. Until limits and model/measurements are reviewed, 5 A continuous operation is unqualified; stop before approved limits or unexpected rising temperature. Hot turn-off SOA is a separate gate.
5. Measure raw output ripple locally with TP504/TP506 or capacitor-terminal spring contact; include probe transfer function and uncertainty. Use rated differential probing for TP113/TP114 and verify common-mode range. Characterize SCR gate/cathode at TP301/TP302 and, for fast edges, at component lands; plane-coupled pads do not guarantee exact Kelvin waveforms.
6. Use a remote dummy load with the intended harness/remote capacitance. Four-wire hot/aged harness round trip ≤25 mΩ; terminal minimum minus uncertainty ≥4.75 V at 5 A, including ripple and load steps. Measure F301 hot resistance, copper/joint drop and connector current sharing. Do not deduct uncertainty from a nominal voltage only.
7. After protection approval, measure source/loop impedance and both raw-bank ESR/ESL; bound Q102 cutoff latency and post-cutoff buck energy. Capture SCR gate rise/current, peak current, di/dt, ∫i²dt, ∫vi dt, remote overshoot, holding/recovery and repeated faults under bounded energy. Manufacturer pulse numbers apply only under their specified waveform/temperature/gate conditions. Validate input OVP, cold-start fault, latch hold/reset and fuse coordination separately.
8. Proceed to Pi integration only after all earlier gates pass and the responsible engineer explicitly approves. Telemetry is N/A in Lite, not a tested function.
