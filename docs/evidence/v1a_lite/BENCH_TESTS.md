# V1A-Lite FIX1 — numbered bench qualification plan

2026-10-05. **STATUS: NOT RUN. Review R1 findings 1–4 remain BLOCKING. NOT FOR FABRICATION — ENGINEERING REVIEW REQUIRED.** This defines tests; it neither authorizes fabrication nor establishes electrical safety. No circuit modifications for findings 1–4 were made. The sequence below preserves the owner brief and AGENTS bring-up gates. A missing acceptance bound is **OPEN/BLOCKED**, never a pass. No Pi until dummy-load, OV/crowbar, cutoff and latch qualification all pass.

## Measurement setup and record

Use a programmable current-limited 0–64 V supply, independently bounded fault-injection fixture, electronic load with transient mode, dummy resistors, calibrated DMM/four-wire ohmmeter, ≥100 MHz scope with ≥1 GS/s capture, appropriately rated differential voltage probes and wideband current probes, thermocouples/thermal camera and controlled hot/cold environment. These bandwidths are minimum equipment selections, not proof of resolving every edge; deskew probes and quantify rise-time/loading uncertainty. Use short Kelvin probe connections, no earth-ground probe clips on S_COMMON/CUT_GATE. Record serial numbers/calibration, probe bandwidth and loading, fixture parasitics, sample/lot, temperature, source limit/rise-time/impedance/hiccup, initial capacitor voltage, harness R/L/C, event count and raw traces. Instrument current at force conductors; **test pads are sense-only**.

| Test access | Net / use |
| --- | --- |
| TP501 / TP502 / TP503 | VIN_RAW / VIN_FUSED / VIN_PROTECTED |
| TP504 / TP505 / TP506 | raw VOUT_5V1 / VOUT_PI / GND; remote voltage also at harness destination |
| TP507 / TP508 | CTRL12 / CB_COLLECTOR (before R307, not SCR gate) |
| TP110 / TP111 / TP112 | SET_LATCH / LATCH_H / IN_NPN_BASE |
| **TP113 / TP114** | S_COMMON / CUT_GATE; differential `VGS(Q102)=TP114−TP113` |
| **TP301 / TP302** | SCR_GATE / SCR cathode GND; adjacent Kelvin pair in future layout |

Actual R3 placement must put TP302 at the SCR cathode sense pickup, paired with TP301, and TP113/114 at Q102 source/gate pickups, using short branches outside force/discharge paths. Common net names do not guarantee Kelvin geometry. U101 K/REF, U301 K/REF, Q101 gate/drain, R307 ends and SCR anode need temporary high-impedance component-pin probes where no dedicated pad exists.

## Ordered tests

1. **Visual inspection, unpowered.** Microscope, assembly drawing and exact-part datasheets: check all packages, polarity, pin numbering, live SCR/FET tabs, solder joints, connector key/carrier, Kelvin routing, switch loops and thermal paths. Check raw SCR has no intervening fuse/link; F301 is only the Pi branch. **PASS:** no discrepancies and package/clearance/thermal review signed. Otherwise stop. PCB layout has not been performed by FIX1.

2. **Continuity/short checks.** DMM/ohmmeter at all listed pads, J501/J502/J503 and harness endpoints; account for charging capacitors and semiconductor junctions. Check F301 removal disconnects J502 positive from raw output but leaves raw bank/SCR/detector together. Confirm both harness + branches reach physical Pi 2/4 only, returns 6/9 only; verify no one-position offset. **PASS:** agrees with released netlist, no unintended shorts, correct diode orientations and keyed fit; four-wire harness resistance consistent with [PI_HARNESS.md](PI_HARNESS.md). Numeric semiconductor resistance thresholds are not invented; record expected direction-dependent readings.

3. **Current-limited first power.** Supply at 18 V, no load, low initial current limit (start 0.1 A as a protective setup choice, not operating requirement). Monitor TP501/503/504/507 and supply current. Increase limit only after explaining initial charging/control behavior. **PASS:** no unexpected sustained limit, smoke, hot spot or uncontrolled output rise; protected rail ≤36 V and raw output <5.5 V including uncertainty. Unexpected limit/hiccup requires diagnosis before continuing.

4. **No-load startup and rail verification.** Repeat at 18/24/26 V with slow ramp and controlled connection. Scope TP503, TP504, TP505, TP507 and TP114−TP113. Measure startup, inrush, overshoot and PFM ripple. **PASS:** stable intended nominal output (~5.065 V), no unwanted latch/crowbar events, steady remote rail ≥4.75 V, all positive excursions <5.5 V including uncertainty. Startup begins at zero; the 4.75 V minimum applies after declared power-valid settling. Maximum settling time/inrush budget remains OPEN and must be approved for release.

5. **Low dummy load, then increasing load steps.** Electronic load at the *remote harness connector*, with intended load capacitance represented; J503 may be used for preliminary board-only checks. Sweep 0.1/0.5/1/2/3/4/5 A at 18/24/26 V. Measure TP504/505 and remote terminal voltage, output/input current, branch sharing and harness drop. **PASS:** settled remote voltage minus uncertainty ≥4.75 V; all peaks plus uncertainty <5.5 V; R_harness ≤40 mΩ hot and each branch ≤3 A or lower qualified rating. No oscillation, unintended trips or sustained current-limit cycling. J502 ≥4.95 V is needed for the selected harness allocation. Record efficiency; the inherited ≥90% proposal is a characterization target, not a new frozen requirement.

6. **Thermal qualification.** Hold each worst-case load/input until temperatures stabilize; start at 25°C, then repeat at the approved minimum/maximum ambient, airflow and enclosure conditions. Ambient endpoints are OPEN (0–50°C is only the earlier proposal). Thermocouples on Q101/102, U201, L201, fuses, TVSs, ballast resistors, SCR, caps, contacts and splices. Record losses and infer junction temperature with a reviewed thermal model. **PASS:** below each manufacturer rating with approved derating, harness housing <85°C, no continuing temperature rise. Proposed semiconductor screen is Tj ≤min(125°C, rated maximum−20°C), pending engineering adoption. Unapproved derating/ambient or unknown Pi-header/contact rating prevents release.

7. **Ripple and normal transient envelope — finding 1, baseline.** At TP504 and remote harness endpoints, use ground-spring/differential probing and 20 MHz bandwidth for steady ripple; also capture full-bandwidth load release, startup/PFM transitions and 0↔5 A load transients with documented slew rate. Characterize actual C209–218, C207/R205 compensation, capacitor tolerance/DC bias and harness R/L/remote C. ≤50 mVpp is an inherited proposed steady-CCM target; light-load and transient limits must be reviewed separately. **PASS for finding 1 baseline:** remote minimum/maximum obey 4.75 V and <5.5 V after settling, stable loop, no nuisance trips; capture a bounded normal-high envelope `Vnormal,max` with uncertainty. Final pass also requires test 8's minimum trip threshold above that envelope. Do not reuse 5.17/5.25 V modeled screens as measured maxima.

8. **Output OV detector and response — finding 1.** Dummy load only. Use a reviewed energy-limited output ramp/step fixture with buck disabled/isolated as appropriate to avoid driving an active supply; then a separately reviewed fixture that represents the buck pass-through fault without altering the production circuit. Sweep source slope/current, temperatures, initial load, capacitor/harness corners and remote capacitance. Simultaneously record TP504, TP505, remote voltage, U301 REF/K, TP508 and TP301−TP302. Measure trip range and detector-to-effective-clamp delay. **PASS:** complete trip interval satisfies `Vtrip,min−uncertainty > Vnormal,max+uncertainty`; remote maximum plus uncertainty **<5.5 V** for every reviewed fault, with raw bank within 10 V bulk/25 V ceramic ratings and approved derating. Measured delay plus uncertainty must fit the worst-case allowed delay computed from the actual source/C/R/L envelope, not the historical 0.416–0.937 µs examples. Normal-range requirements still apply before the fault; undervoltage shutdown during fault is permitted. An unbounded source or threshold/delay corner is BLOCKED.

9. **Input OV cold-start and running cutoff — finding 2.** First characterize the upstream source's short current, impedance, rise rate and hiccup using a rated dummy fixture. After its envelope is reviewed, apply controlled 54.6 V and 64 V cold connections and 18/24/26 V→54.6/64 V running steps; progress from limited energy to the reviewed worst case, never assume a bench supply's limit bounds local capacitor discharge. Capture TP501/502/503, TP507, TP113/114, U101 REF/K, TP112 and Q102 drain current; probe Q101 VGS/VDS too. Measure detector/cutoff timing, Q102 VDS×ID pulse energy and hot SOA, ballast/pullup pulse stress, protected-bank peak/reversal and TVS temperatures. **PASS:** VIN_PROTECTED plus uncertainty ≤36 V throughout this conservative screen (42 V is LM61460 absolute maximum, not an operating allowance), both FET |VGS| <20 V and |VDS| <150 V with approved margin, capacitors/TVSs/resistors within reviewed transient/thermal limits, measured hot IRFR6215 SOA satisfied, and no continuous TVS dissipation beyond rating. Any proposed >36 V transient requires separate datasheet-based engineering acceptance; ≥42 V fails. Continuous fault dwell and maximum cutoff delay must be derived from the approved source/thermal envelope; they remain OPEN until bounded. Use actual IRFR6215 data, not old IRF9540 results.

10. **Trigger capture, latch hold and reset — finding 3.** During the real crowbar events measure the shortest TP508 pulse and TP110/111/112, TP507, TP114−TP113 and TP503/504. Then use a calibrated pulse fixture through the existing set path to establish the longest required minimum set-pulse width at hot/cold and low CTRL12. Sweep supply dips, source hiccup and intentional input-removal intervals; discharge stored energy between prescribed cases. **PASS:** minimum real set pulse minus uncertainty exceeds worst-case required capture width plus uncertainty; Q106 inhibits Q103 and Q102 stops forward conduction, stays off after output collapse and throughout every permitted source dip/hiccup, and never autonomously restarts. Record a bounded intentional input-removal reset interval and verify repeatability. Digital logic levels alone are insufficient: verify Q102 current and VGS. Exact permitted dips and reset time remain OPEN; if a permitted hiccup resets it, this fails and needs a later circuit review.

11. **SCR gate/discharge stress and recovery — finding 4.** Coordinate captures with tests 8–10 using TP301/302, TP508, TP504 and a calibrated current probe in the SCR anode conductor. Gate current is `[(V(TP508)−V(TP301))/R307] − [V(TP301−TP302)/R308]`, accounting for resistor tolerance, probe loading and dynamic parasitics. Measure gate amplitude/rise, anode peak, di/dt, integrated I²t and v×i energy; include both capacitor reservoirs and ongoing source current until Q102 opens. **PASS:** trigger/latching margin at every temperature, and all gate/anode/thermal/recovery stresses within waveform-appropriate ST TYN612RG limits. The 25°C RG trigger maximum is 15 mA (not TRG's 5 mA); the **50 A/µs** turn-on limit is conditioned on **IG=2×IGT and tr≤100 ns**. Establish those conditions or obtain a supported alternate bound; do not call 35 mA modeled drive sufficient at all temperatures. Catalog surge/I²t values apply only to their specified initial temperature/pulse shape and cannot directly qualify an arbitrary capacitor pulse. Full-temperature trigger bound, waveform derating and safe repetition/reset interval remain OPEN. Verify current falls below holding level and recovery completes before reapplication; no damage/parameter drift after the approved repeat count. Never insert a fuse between SCR and raw bank; F301 cannot clear that local discharge.

12. **Remaining fault/protection and telemetry gate.** Dummy fixture only: reverse input/backfeed, output short/overload, open F301, connector single-contact loss and source restart. Measure TP501/503/504/505 and force currents/temperatures. **PASS:** no unintended forward bypass, unacceptable backfeed or rating violation; fuse total-clearing behavior coordinates with every conductor/contact under the actual limited/hiccup source. Numeric leakage limits, clearing envelope and contact-loss mitigation remain OPEN and block release until reviewed. Telemetry is **N/A**, removed in Lite, not “validated”.

13. **Pi integration — last gate only.** Requires signed evidence for 1–12, closed OPEN acceptance bounds, harness key/current-rating/thermal qualification, and all four review blockers closed. Recheck continuity/polarity without Pi, then connect Pi with other power sources absent. Apply the settings in PI_HARNESS.md only for the qualified current capability. Record Pi workload/USB load, firmware, undervoltage flags, actual remote rail and connector temperatures. **PASS:** intended workload operates within the qualified current/voltage/thermal envelope; it does not retroactively validate any earlier fault test.
